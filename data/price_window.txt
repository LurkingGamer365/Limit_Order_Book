# the book's price window is 0 to 65535 inclusive

# prices exactly on the edges are accepted and rest
A 1 B 0 10
A 2 S 65535 10

# one tick outside each edge is rejected and never rests
A 3 B -1 10
A 4 S 65536 10

# far outside the window, the "buy at 1" case from the other direction
A 5 S 99999 10

# rejected orders were never in the book, so cancelling them is rejected too
C 3
C 4

# normal orders still work and can trade against the edge orders
A 6 S 0 5
A 7 B 65535 5
